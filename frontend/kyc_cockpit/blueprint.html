Edge Quality Pre-Filter (On-Device Wasm)

⚙️ What Happens in this Step:

When a citizen or kiosk agent points their smartphone camera at an Iraqi ID card, an on-device WebAssembly worker analyzes the live video stream at 60 FPS before uploading over cellular data. It runs two real-time algorithms: Laplacian variance for motion blur (σ² ≥ 150) and luminance thresholding for specular flash glare (>245 RGB covering >5% area).

📥 Input Data:

Raw 1080p camera frames from customer mobile browser or kiosk tablet

📤 Output Data:

Clean, glare-free, non-blurred frame (JPEG) + Quality Confidence Score (0-100%)

🏆 The Competitive Hackathon Differentiator:

Saves 75% of server bandwidth and prevents rejected submissions before they reach the cloud. Competitors upload blurry images to cloud LLMs, wasting quota and creating frustrating retry loops.

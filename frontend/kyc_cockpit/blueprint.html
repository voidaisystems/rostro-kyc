Stage 01 of 07

Edge Quality Gate (Laplacian & Specular Glare Filter)

Pre-Ingestion Quality Gate

Technical Specification

Before transmitting document captures to the extraction pipeline, Rostro evaluates optical clarity, motion blur (Laplacian variance), and specular flash glare on plastic laminate cards. Captures below threshold trigger immediate retake guidance rather than downstream OCR guessing.

Input Specification

Raw document image frame (JPEG/PNG) from mobile client or reviewer upload

Output Specification

Optical quality score (0.00–1.00) + deterministic quality flags (SPECULAR_FLASH_GLARE, BLUR)

Operational & Compliance Value

Eliminates garbage-in/garbage-out OCR errors caused by indoor fluorescent reflections on Iraqi National ID cards and laminated Chamber of Commerce licenses.

Architectural Invariant

Never fabricate or interpolate characters covered by specular glare. Occluded mandatory fields are preserved as null and routed to retake or human compliance review.
